Video principle (Nick 12:15 AM): extract features on the fly (numbers, timestamps), never store raw video.

- Tokens: tokens = events x 6 for event streams (noggin_tokens vocab v1). Three kinds, counted separately (Nick 11:50 PM): 'events' = new real plays/pitches/balls; 'context-new' = external facts we did not have (odds, weather, refs, injuries, TV slot), counted ONCE per game as unique tokens, with as-fed tokens (repeated on every play by vocab v2) shown only in the why line; 'derived' = computed from data we already hold (WP, leverage, momentum, timeouts left, lineups, player-to-date, standings and playoff odds, rest and travel): adds tokens per play but no new knowledge. Synthetic and augmentation (mirror, re-timing) are never in this file; they are capped at 3x real in data-ledger.md.
- Rank: score = new_tokens_m x novelty / raw_gb. novelty 1.5 = adds wall-clock timing we lack; 1.0 = new sport, league or level; 0.5 = more of a league we already hold.
- Every **yellow-B** row is tagged **"personal-use, not for commercial release"** on the dataset and on any model trained on it; the company version retrains on green/yellow only.
- Guardrails: public endpoints only; no logins, paywalls, CAPTCHAs or bot-block bypasses; robots and rate limits respected; derived numbers only from articles or pages; no broadcast video or audio; any paid feed needs Nick's OK; jobs run on Kaggle/Colab, never the Mac.
