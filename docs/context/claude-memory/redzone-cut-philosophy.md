Nick's top priorities for the auto-switching TV app (stated 2026-11-02, after the first live night):
- Leave a game only during dead time: free throws, timeouts, official reviews, injuries.
- Enter a game right as play resumes (out of a timeout, inbound, snap), never mid-play. "Preload at a point, then press resume" is an idea he likes.
- NFL gets priority when a drive is alive (moving the ball, red zone). Punt-fests drop.
- Do RedZone-style catch-up: rewinds and "here's what you missed" highlight runs.
- He judges the app by key moments caught. Skipping key moments is the failure he notices most. He wants many variables in the hype score.

**Why:** the beta proved switching works; he now wants it "INSANE" — correct cut points and the right game every time.

**How to apply:** measure every change with mid-play cuts on both leave and entry (must be 0), key moments seen and time behind live. He gave open authority to research, pull GitHub repos and run hardware tests overnight, and said never to stop working on it. Related: [[redzone-no-peacock]], [[no-build-until-explicit-go]].
