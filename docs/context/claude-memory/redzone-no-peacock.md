For RedZone Everything (the auto-switching TV app), Nick says Peacock is never needed: anything on Peacock is also on NBC / NBC Sports Network on YouTube TV, so route those games through YouTube TV.

**Why:** Peacock is signed in on only one of the two Fire TV sticks and its video can't be screenshotted, so delay can't be measured automatically; YouTube TV delay is measured automatically from show changes.

**How to apply:** rank YouTube TV NBC/NBCSN above Peacock in provider routing; the blocker (as of 2026-11-02) is that the YouTube TV watch codes for NBCSN and the local NBC station aren't known yet. Related: [[no-build-until-explicit-go]].
