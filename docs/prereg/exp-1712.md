# EXP-1712 / EXP-1713 pre-registration: STAY or LEAVE from full game context

Lane 1 (T8 of Nick's 10:00 AM Nov 5 order). Committed BEFORE any model is fit or graded (Nov 5 2026).
A change after this commit needs a new EXP id ([grading.md](../grading.md) rule 2).
Spec: [Watch Value v2 §2b and §5](../plans/2026-11-05-watch-value-v2.md).

## Hypothesis (Nick, 9:45 AM Nov 5)

STAY vs LEAVE must come from the full game context, not the single play. A per-game **state + consequence**
model that predicts each live game's watch value over the next minutes, past the end of what the buffer
reveals, makes better stay/leave decisions than the windowed oracle's v1 placeholder (each game's mean reward
over its last 10 known minutes, `oracle.beyond_value`).

**North Star line:** % of oracle (`pct_of_oracle`). Secondary: regret vs the full-hindsight oracle,
crunch-exit accuracy, switches/hour, dead air, big plays and crunch plays seen live.

## The decision and the hard floors (not learned)

The policy is `oracle.solve_window` (exact DP over what the buffer reveals, replan every 5 s step) with its
beyond-window value swapped. The floors bind every arm, including the baseline:

- **Never join mid-play** and never cut mid-play: the oracle's own `land` / `leave` tables, unchanged.
- **Never leave a high-leverage moment:** no switch away from a game whose last play on screen is
  `leverage.high_leverage` (close and late, or overtime). One such leave in any graded night FAILS the row.

## The model (fixed now)

- **Inputs, per live game, known at the end of the buffer window only** (plays whose screen step is at or before
  the window's last step; nothing later):
  - state: game clock left (fraction of regulation), overtime, period, |margin| scaled by the league's score
    spread, closeness 1 - |2 WP - 1|, suspense (WP change one score either way would cause), high-leverage
    flag, comeback odds (the trailing side's WP);
  - consequence of the recent plays: |ΔWP| of the last play, |ΔWP| over 5 min, WP volatility over 10 min,
    margin swing over 5 min (a run, entered only as measured WP movement);
  - attention-free history: big plays per min (5 and 15 min), crunch plays per min (5 min), dead-air share
    (5 min), the v1 past-mean reward (10 min);
  - stakes available today: your-team flag (WSH/UNC). Standings/playoff stakes join when B's table is wired.
  - WP is the label-only Brownian model in `outcome_label.win_prob` (a fixed formula; no learned WP).
- **Label:** the realized reward that game would pay over the next **300 s** of its screen time
  (`oracle.OBJECTIVES["combined"]`: big + crunch + your-team crunch - dead air), from real play-by-play.
  Not a teacher output, not Nick's viewing or buttons, not an engagement label.
- **Learner:** sklearn `HistGradientBoostingRegressor`, squared loss, learning_rate 0.05, max_leaf_nodes 15,
  min_samples_leaf 40, l2 0.1. `max_iter` picked on VALID only from {100, 200, 400}. One model pooled over
  NBA + NFL + NHL with a league one-hot. Seeds 0, 1, 2 (the model's own random state), averaged.
- Rows: every 30 s of screen time per live game on TRAIN nights, buffer B = 60 s (screen lag 86 s,
  feed lag 8 s, tune 5 s, the sweep's defaults).

## Arms (same nights, same floors, same grader)

| Arm | Beyond-window value | Hysteresis |
|---|---|---|
| A0 baseline | v1 past mean (`oracle.beyond_value`) | none |

Reported beside them, not an arm: the full-hindsight oracle (`oracle.solve`, the 100 %) and the windowed
oracle with no floor (to show what the floor costs).

## Metrics (per league, per arm)

- **% of oracle** = sum over nights of `schedule_value(arm)` / sum of `schedule_value(full oracle)`, objective
  `combined`. **Regret** = full - arm, per night.
- **High-leverage leaves**: switches whose from-game is high-leverage at that step. Must be 0.
- **Mid-play entries** (`sportsgrade` card). Must be 0.
- **Crunch-exit accuracy**: of the leaves from a game with a crunch play in the 5 min before, the share after
  which that game paid no big or crunch play in the next 5 min.
- switches/hour, dead_air_pct, big_live, crunch_live.
- Night-cluster bootstrap, 2000 resamples, 95 % CI of A1 - A0 and A2 - A1 on % of oracle.

## Success bar

- **EXP-1712 PASS:** A1 - A0 on % of oracle >= +1.0 pt pooled over leagues with CI excluding 0, no league
  worse by more than its CI, **0 high-leverage leaves, 0 mid-play entries**, crunch-exit accuracy not lower
  than A0's, switches/hour not up by more than 10 %.
- **EXP-1713 PASS:** A2 - A1 >= 0 within CI with switches/hour down >= 10 %, or A2 - A1 >= +1.0 pt with CI
  excluding 0; same floors.
- Train/valid gap over 5 pts of % of oracle = overfit: report it and simplify, do not tune.
- A FAIL ships nothing. A PASS ships flag-off (`decider.stay_leave.learned`, default off) until the release gate.
