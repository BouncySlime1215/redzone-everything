# EXP-027 pre-registration: surprise drives replays, never cut-ins

Registered Nov 4 2026, ~20:15 ET (Nov 5 01:15 UTC), by the EXP-027 builder, **before any run**. This file is
committed before `scripts/exp027_surprise.py` is first run on real nights. Nothing below changes after
the first run; a changed bar becomes a new EXP id (grading rule 2).

## Hypothesis (queue, verbatim)

> Surprise drives replays and never cut-ins. KL surprise over the full outcome distribution handles OT
> and shootouts; a rarity bonus comes later from the world model.

## North Star line

- **Moves:** Big plays live or replayed within 3 min.
- **Must not move:** Mid-play entries (unchanged, 0). Crunch live (no worse).

## What is built

- `redzone/noggin/surprise.py`. Surprise of a missed big play = KL(after ‖ before) in bits over six final
  outcomes: home or away winning in regulation, in OT, or in a shootout. Before and after are the score just
  before and just after the play, at the play's own clock. The distributions come from the analytic WP
  models in `redzone/scoring/gameflow.py`: normal margin for NBA/CBB, the same model on a stretched clock
  for NFL/CFB (as `scoring.brain.analytic_wp`), and Poisson for NHL regulation. NHL 3-on-3 OT uses a fixed
  rate (60 % of OTs decided), then the shootout.
- It only adds `20 points/bit × min(bits, 2)` to `Missed.value` of a missed big play that is already in
  the Decider's catch-up queue. That changes only which replay goes first and whether a replay clears
  the live-play bar. Live candidates and every stay/switch rule are untouched. No live behavior changes:
  the hook is installed only by the harness, and it is a no-op unless `noggin.surprise.enabled` is true
  (OFF; the key is not in `default_config.yaml`).
- Out of scope: the world-model rarity bonus, and turnovers with no score change (the analytic WP sees
  only score and clock, so they read 0 bits).
- The knobs are fixed here: 20 points/bit, cap 2 bits. There is no sweep and no tuning on any split.

## Protocol

- **Nights:** dev only. Split `fit` (train + valid) of the pinned window (`redzone/lab/splits.py`) per
  league, NFL/NBA/NHL. The harness refuses `test`. **Locked nights are not read.**
- **Runs:** the real backtest (`redzone/lab/backtest.run`) with the scorecards' honest knobs (measured
  feed lag, 6 s tune jitter). Seeds 0, 1 and 2. Baseline is the pinned eval config; the candidate is the
  baseline plus `noggin.surprise.enabled=true`. Nothing else differs.
- **Grader:** the frozen scorecards (`sportsgrade.grade` for NBA/NHL, the `Report` for NFL), graded
  against real play-by-play.

## Success bar

For each league:

1. **Lift:** Δ = candidate − baseline on big_seen_3m / big, in points.
   - The seed mean must be **≥ +2.0**.
   - The paired night-bootstrap 95 % CI lower bound must be **> 0**. Nights are resampled whole, NFL
     slates are clustered by night, counts are averaged over seeds, and there are 2000 resamples.
2. **Guardrails (all must hold):**
   - entries_mid_play is not above baseline (it stays 0).
   - **0 switches are started by surprise outside a replay.** This is counted at run time as any
     non-replay decision that carries a surprise-valued replay, and it is proven in
     `tests/test_exp027_surprise.py`.
   - Crunch live is not below baseline.
3. **Power and calibration:**
   - A league with fewer than 8 dev nights is reported but marked **PENDING**.
   - A league whose WP misses EXP-002's ECE ≤ 0.05 is also reported but marked **PENDING**. EXP-002's dev
     numbers are NFL 0.047, NBA 0.047 and NHL 0.116, so **NHL is PENDING whatever it shows**.

**EXP-027 passes when the bar and guardrails hold in at least 2 of NFL/NBA/NHL.** A failing run ships
nothing on. The flag stays OFF and the module stays harness-only.

## Known risks, stated before running

- Few fit nights for NBA and NHL (about 10 and 11), so the CIs will be wide. EXP-002's NBA ECE rests on 2
  valid nights.
- A larger replay value can only add replays where the queue already holds a big play and the live bar
  allows one, so the lift is capped by the replay opportunities the current catch-up rules allow.
- The NFL WP model is a basketball model on a stretched clock with no down, distance or field position.
  Surprise there is about the score only.
