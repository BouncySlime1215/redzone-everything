# EXP-023 pre-registration: belief tracker

Committed BEFORE the full evaluation run (Nov 4 2026). Changes after this commit need a new EXP id
([grading.md](../grading.md) rule 2).

## Hypothesis (queue, verbatim)

A belief tracker (a particle filter per game for feed and TV lag, with online Bayesian updates of each
broadcast's break rhythm) cuts landing error.

**North Star lines:** Per-stick delay error p95; Mid-play entries.

## What runs

- **Candidate:** `redzone/noggin/belief.py` (`BeliefBook` in place of the Kalman `SyncBook`), behind flag
  `noggin.belief.enabled`, OFF. Nothing live reads it; the harness `scripts/exp023_belief.py` swaps it in.
- **Baseline:** `DelayBook` + its Kalman sync (`redzone/decider/sync.py`), the flag-on break-anchor path.
- **Same observations for both arms.** The backtest stress knobs `--true-delay 41 --lag-jitter --real-lag`,
  plus the live break anchors (`sync.break_anchor.enabled=true`, the only automatic delay signal the
  backtest models today) and 6 s tune jitter (`--tune-jitter 6`). Without anchors neither arm sees any
  observation and the two are identical by construction (a sanity line, not the test).
- **Seeds:** 0, 1, 2.
- **Parameters:** all fixed in `BeliefParams` before the run (300 particles, tune jitter 6 s, prior
  mixture 20% at the config's 15 s default doubt, rhythm prior 4 pseudo-anchors). None is tuned on any
  split.

## Nights

- **NBA and NHL:** every `fit` (train + valid) night of the pinned window: 9 NBA, 10 NHL.
- **NFL:** the `valid` nights only (11). Reason: compute. The full NFL `fit` set is 56 Sundays x 3 seeds x
  2 arms, about 6 hours on the Mac the night the live app runs. `valid` is still dev data and above the
  8-night minimum.
- **Locked (`test`) nights are not read.** The harness refuses `--split test`.

## Success bar (all must hold, per league: NFL, NBA, NHL)

1. The tracker's simulated delay error p95 (the backtest `honesty` rows, |error| of the delay used on every
   on-screen tick, pooled over nights and seeds) beats the baseline by **>= 2.0 s**, and the night-cluster
   bootstrap 95% CI of the delta (2000 resamples of whole nights, 3 seeds pooled within a night) excludes 0.
2. **Rule #1:** candidate `entries_mid_play` = 0 and high-leverage leaves (`cuts_high`) = 0.
3. Landings in dead time and `plays_missed_entry` no worse: the CI of the delta includes 0 or favors the
   candidate.
4. Under 8 dev nights in a league = **PENDING** for that league.

Real per-stick delay error p95 stays **PENDING** (needs live nights) and is report-only.
Feed-lag tracking (|predicted - realized| per play, vs the fixed 8 s the app assumes, drawn from the
measured profile) is report-only.

## Disclosure

Before this commit one plumbing smoke run was made: one NBA `train` night (20261020), seed 0. It showed the
tracker learning the channel (46 s vs a kickoff 43 s, truth about 52 s) but its estimate gated out by
sync.py's "surer than the predictor" rule (the stressed kickoff states sigma 3 s). The gate was dropped
(`BeliefBook.estimate`: the predictor is the cloud's prior, so the posterior already holds it). No
parameter value was changed.
