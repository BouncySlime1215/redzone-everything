# EXP-603: hidden-stick readiness with the overlay-scrub entry (Nov 7, 2026)

Owner T9 (Nick's order, Nov 6 10:15 PM, item 5). The grader is frozen by the integrator before any run.

## Why
EXP-601 charged every buffered switch a tune plus a B-second pause on the hidden stick (~97-120 s). Nick's Nov 6
stick tests showed a pause does not hold a buffer at all, but an overlay scrub does
(docs/sync/2026-11-06-yttv-scrub-buffer-spec.md and the Nov 6 update in docs/sync/2026-11-05-yttv-live-edge-tests.md,
codex/resume-redzone a60c9cbd). The withdrawn 75 s / 79.66 s number (EXP-602, void) is not used.

## Model (measured values only)
- `backtest.run(..., hidden_stick=..., hs_entry="scrub")`, `eval_sports.py --hs-entry scrub`. Default `hold` is
  EXP-601 byte for byte.
- Ready time after the tune = keys 10 s (CENTER, N x LEFT, CENTER over adb, spec) + one label read 8 s
  (CENTER, LEFT, RIGHT, CENTER) = **18 s**, whatever B. Prime still holds nothing (ready at the tune).
- Where it lands: N=4 read 67 s behind on TNT, and each read adds ~8 s (67, 75, 83 at 1.3, 10.3, 20.3 min), so a
  scrub plus one read airs **15 N + 15 s** behind live. Only B = 75 (N=4) and B = 90 (N=5) are graded; any other B
  raises.
- Tune: the Nov 4 lognormal (EXP-601, unchanged). Everything else in HiddenStick unchanged: one preload at a time,
  planner-picked; the stick we left keeps the game we left; a game that aired at the live edge must re-enter (18 s).

## Runs (one pass, no tuning on these numbers)
- eval-data-v1, `--split fit` only (eval_sports refuses `--hidden-stick` on locked). `--real-lag`.
- NBA first: B90 scrub, dwell `decider.dwell.long_visit_min_score=10` (as EXP-601). Then NFL: B75 scrub,
  `decider.dwell.min_s=35`. Controls: B0 with the same arm, `--hs-entry hold` (EXP-601's control).
- Both arms (wait, live_edge), both harnesses (shipped, resync), seeds 0, 1, 2. NBA live_edge also through
  entry_autopsy + clock_truth_grade (both NBA graders).
- Report per arm: entries_mid_play, plays_missed_entry, dead_air_pct, non-replay switches and per hour, big and
  crunch live, your-team crunch live, % switches buffered, wait s, Prime switches at B = 0 and their mid-play entries.

## Pass (per league)
- Rule #1: 0 mid-play entries added vs the B0 control, every seed, both harnesses, both NBA graders.
- Switch guardrail: non-replay switches <= 1.5x the control.
- Plays missed on entry and dead air not worse than the control (the PR rule), else a measured reason.
- **Both arms must pass.** Live control.py airs an unready target at the live edge (EXP-601 citation); Nick's
  item 4 (seek the hidden stick before the flip) would make it wait. Until item 4 ships, which arm is real is
  ambiguous, so the verdict needs both.
- The rest of the RC8 gate (docs/plans/2026-11-05-rc8-buffer-gate.md) unchanged; locked + shadow + sync still pend.
  A pass here is not a flip card: Nick's rule stands that RC8 is not scheduled until a stick test proves the hold
  live (flip onto a scrubbed stick, a person looking at the picture).
- A fail is logged; B and dwell are not re-tuned on these nights. A new point needs a new EXP id.

## Not modelled (disclosed)
Ads during the hold, the per-channel back limit (~2.5 min on ACC Network), session timeouts, a failed scrub
(fail closed = live edge, i.e. arm live_edge), and key timing faster than adb (5 s by hand).

## Integrator freeze notes (v40, Nov 7)
