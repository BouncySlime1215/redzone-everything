# EXP-1725: reachable crunch (NBA), from the mistake ledger (WV loop cycle 6)

Status: pre-registered 2026-11-06 ~12:00Z, before any read of eval-data-v3's new nights. personal-use, not for commercial release.

## Why (cycle 6 diagnosis, NBA fit nights, 90 s buffer, p90 feed lag, full oracle, combined objective)
`scripts/ledger_decomp.py` splits every ledger episode's value lost into its parts:
- **54% of the value lost is dead-air steps** (0.02 each), and only 46% is plays: 8 big plays and 23 crunch plays over 9 nights.
- 42 of 46 episodes lose no plays at all. All of the play loss sits in 4 episodes.
- Two of those 4 come from the crunch floor holding a game that was already decided:
  - Oct 26: up 7 with 0:06 left. The app held it 3.5 min while a 1-point game played its last 1:03 (4 big + 5 crunch lost).
  - Oct 26: an OT game held to its last seconds while another game was down 3 at 2:02.
- So `wrong_game_value` (64% of the value lost) is mostly dead-air bookkeeping. A value-weighted ledger weights dead air, which is why B2 ≈ B1 (EXP-1723).

- Base A0: as shipped.
