# EXP-025 pre-registration: arrive at the dead ball before the big play

Committed on branch `claude/exp-025` (base `origin/integration/next` 82ae167) on 2026-11-04, BEFORE the
first run of `scripts/exp025_prebig.py`. Nothing below changes after the first run. A change means a new
EXP id ([grading.md](../grading.md) rule 2).

## Hypothesis (queue, verbatim)

> Arrive at the dead ball BEFORE the big play: predict the next high-leverage snap and switch at the
> stoppage before it. Measure: seconds of build-up seen per big play.

- **North Star lines:** Big plays seen live (NFL, NBA, NHL); Landings in dead time.
- **Queue source:** P7 ([experiments.md](../experiments.md) row 25).

## What is tested

`redzone/noggin/prebig.py`, behind the flag `noggin.prebig.enabled`. The flag is **OFF** by default and is
not in `default_config.yaml`. No live code builds the stage. Only the lab harness
(`redzone/lab/buildup.py: prebig_runs`) swaps it in for scorecards.

While the app would stay, the stage looks for another game whose next snap is high-leverage. It reads the
scorer's own `pre_moment`/`lock` and a crunch run-up from the timeline clock, and asks to switch there.
The decider's leave gate and its whistle wait on entry then land the switch on a stoppage. Once on that
game, the stage holds through the build-up while the signal lasts, capped at 240 s for NFL and NBA and
180 s for NHL.

It never leaves:
- your team's game;
- a crunch window;
- a locked moment.

It never overrides a crunch switch, a user switch or the trip home.

**Fixed constants.** These are hand-set priors, not fitted, and they are not tuned after any run:

| Constant | Value |
|---|---|
| `HAZARD_PRE` | as in the module at this commit |
| `HAZARD_LOCK` | as in the module at this commit |
| `RUNUP` | as in the module at this commit |
| `MIN_HAZARD` | 0.3 |
| `MARGIN` | 0.15 |
| `PREBIG_MIN_GAP` | 60 s |
| `PREBIG_HOLD` | NFL 240 s, NBA 240 s, NHL 180 s |

## Design

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Nights | **DEV only**: the `fit` split (train + valid) of the pinned `eval-data-v1` window ([splits.py](../../redzone/lab/splits.py)). NBA all dev nights (9 in the split); NHL all dev nights (10 in the split); NFL the newest 20 of its 56 dev nights (`--max-nights 20`). The NFL cap bounds load on the light Mac and is fixed here, before any run. Each night needs at least 2 games in the archive (`eval_sports.nights`). |
| Locked nights | `test` is **not read** by this lane. The script refuses `--split test`. Locked numbers come only from the integrator's release run (report-only). |
| Seeds | 0, 1, 2. The measured feed-lag model (`feed_lag_model="measured"`, the North Star cards' `--real-lag`) is the seeded noise. |
| Arms | Paired. Each night and seed is replayed twice, as control (the app as it ships on this base) and as prebig (the flag on). Same harness: `backtest.run` + `sportsgrade.grade`, tune 5 s, `data/eval_config.yaml` with shipped switch costs. |
| Unit of evidence | The night. Per night, counts are summed over seeds. Deltas are pooled-rate (prebig − control) with a **night-cluster bootstrap 95% CI** (2000 draws, seed 7, paired nights). |

## Success bar (primary)

PASS needs all of the following:

1. **Big plays seen live** rises by **≥ +2.0 pts**. This is the seed mean of the per-seed pooled delta.
2. The CI lower bound of the delta is **> 0**.
3. Items 1 and 2 hold in **at least 2 of NFL / NBA / NHL**.
4. No league is worse beyond its CI. That means no league's delta CI lies wholly below 0.

## Guards (Rule #1 and no-worse)

| Guard | Bar |
|---|---|
| `entries_mid_play` | **0** in the prebig arm |
| High-leverage mid-play leaves (`cuts_high`) | **0** in the prebig arm |
| Dead air | No worse: the CI of the delta includes 0 or is better |
| `plays_missed_entry` per 100 landings | No worse: the CI of the delta includes 0 or is better |

A league that breaks a guard is a FAIL, and that fails the experiment.

## Status rules

- A league with **under 8 nights** is **PENDING** and does not count toward the 2 leagues.
- The overall result is **PENDING** when the passing leagues plus the PENDING leagues could still reach 2 and no league failed.

## Secondary (info only)

**Median seconds of build-up seen per big play seen live.** This is continuous on-screen time of that game
before the play airs, capped at 180 s. It is computed in `redzone/lab/buildup.py`, the experiment's own
module, not a frozen grader. It also covers the count of big plays with ≥ 20 s of build-up and "Crunch live"
as a context line. The secondary line is expected to rise, but it is labeled secondary and never decides
PASS or FAIL.

## Known limits, stated up front

- NFL is graded per night with `sportsgrade` (the `eval_sports` harness), not per 1 pm / 4 pm slate as in
  `scripts/eval.py`. Both arms use the same harness, so the paired delta is like for like.
- The hazards are rule priors. A learned hazard (Noggin Predict v1 exists for NBA/NHL) is a follow-up
  under a new EXP id.
