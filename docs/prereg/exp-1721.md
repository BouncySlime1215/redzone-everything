# EXP-1721: oracle reward v2 (big-play definitions + objective weights), pre-registered

Status: PROPOSED, for the integrator to freeze (it changes gates; log in decisions.md). Nothing in RC6.3 or integration/release changes.
Tag: personal-use, not for commercial release. Evidence: docs/audits/oracle-2026-11-05/ (train/fit seasons only, locked never read).

## Why (audit, 2026-11-05)
- NBA: is_big = non-FT score in the last 2:00 of Q4 within 5, and graded crunch = Q4 2:00 within 5 or OT. Both are the same
  close-and-late window, so the oracle scores ONLY close finishes. 2021-24 regular season (4,770 games): 57.5% of games are worth 0;
  per-game oracle value vs final margin Spearman -0.83; games decided by <= 5 (26% of games) get 73% of oracle value but hold 37% of
  WP excitement; 74% of the top-1% WP-swing plays happen before the last 2:00 and score 0. Hypothesis "the grader structurally
  favors closest-game": CONFIRMED.
- NFL: is_big = every scoring play or turnover. 2023-24 REG (nflverse wpa, PATs excluded, 544 games): 31% of big plays move WP < 2
  points, 15.5% happen with the game decided (pre-play WP < 5% or > 95%), and 38% of the top-1% WP-swing plays are not big
  (long completions, 4th-down stops, missed FGs, punt plays). Count of big plays ranks games by WP excitement 54.4 vs 79.1 for swing.
- OBJECTIVES['combined'] = (3, 1, 100, 0.02, 0.5) are hand-picked with no fit and mix units (counts vs steps).
- Market volume is NOT a usable judge between rule sets: v1 62.6 = v2 62.5 vs volume, and realized closeness reads 65.6 (volume
  itself favors close finishes). The judge must be play-level human signals (recaps, highlight reels).

## New definitions (frozen here)
- big_v2(play) = pre-play WP in [0.05, 0.95] AND ( |dWP| >= p99 of league |dWP| on fit seasons [NBA 0.127, NFL from nflverse wpa]
  OR (scoring play or turnover AND |dWP| >= 0.02) OR (NBA: v1 is_big) ).
- crunch: unchanged (crunch.graded); a game with no crunch still earns big_v2.
- reward per 5 s step = sum over live big_v2 plays of |dWP| (WP units) + w_c * crunch play + 100 * your-team crunch - w_d * dead step
  - w_s * switch. w_c, w_d, w_s fit ONCE on fit nights to maximize agreement of oracle-shown plays with the human highlight set
  (recaps + highlight reels, step 1b), then frozen; until then w_c = 0.05 WP per crunch play, w_d = 0.002, w_s = 0.02.

## Acceptance (graded once, fit nights; locked untouched)
1. Share of NBA games with zero oracle value drops below 20%.
2. Recall of the human highlight set (step 1b) by oracle-shown plays beats v1 by >= 10 points, night-bootstrap CI > 0.
3. NFL: big_v2 plays in decided game state = 0 by construction; top-1% swing recall >= 95%.
Fail on 2 -> keep v1 and log it; no tuning on the same nights.
