# EXP-032 pre-registration: red-team search for nights that break the brain

**Committed before the first evaluation run** on archive nights (the run's commit follows this one).

- **Queue row (verbatim):** "Red-team agents hunt for nights that break the brain, extending the night lab."
- **North Star lines:** Faults recovered; Mid-play entries.
- **Kind:** report-only lab tool. No live change, no default change. Flag: `redzone.lab.redteam.ENABLED = False`,
  and `scripts/redteam.py` does nothing without `--enable`.

## What is searched

The backtest's stress knobs (`redzone/lab/backtest.run`), as a box in unit space:

| knob | range | base (clean) |
|---|---|---|
| true_delay (app believes 35 s) | 20-55 s | 35 |
| sigma | 1-8 s | 3 |
| lag_jitter low edge | 0-10 s | 0 |
| lag_jitter span | 0-40 s | 0 |
| drop (silences per game-minute) | 0-0.3 | 0 |
| late_plays | 0-0.4 | 0 |
| no_wallclock | 0-1 | 0 |
| tune_jitter_s | 0-8 s | 0 |
| seed | 0-9999 | 0 |

The **fixed stress grid** it must beat is `redteam.FIXED_GRID`: the 12 mixes `tests/test_midplay.py` and
`replaycheck`'s combined_faults already run.

Search (deterministic given its seed): edges (base, each axis alone at max, all at max), then uniform random
to 60 % of the budget, then Gaussian local steps around the three worst mixes. Guide: failures, then any
mid-play leave, then plays missed on entry.

## Failure definition (what "breaks the brain" means)

On one night under one mix, graded against the TRUE screen delay (as `scripts/eval_sports.py` does):
`entries_mid_play > 0` **or** `cuts_high > 0` (a mid-play leave at a high-leverage moment, `lab/leverage.py`).
Plain mid-play leaves are reported as a secondary line, not a failure.

## Success bars

**(a) Planted recovery (unit test, no archive).** On the 7 planted known-bad regions in
`tests/test_redteam.py::PLANTED` (5 hard boxes with no near-miss signal, 2 narrow soft ones), `search()` with
budget **120 evaluations** and search seed 0 recovers **100 %** (7 of 7). Below 7 of 7 = fail.

**(b) Dev nights.** Nights: the newest **5 dev nights** (train + valid, `splits.py`, pinned window end) of
**NBA** and of **NHL** = 10 nights. NFL is out of scope for this run (about 15 s per evaluation on the light Mac).
Locked (test) nights are refused by code and never read. Budget: **48 search evaluations per night**
(480 total), plus the 12 grid mixes per night, plus 3 reruns and 3 neighbouring seeds for at most 3 distinct
finds per night. Search seed 0.

- **Found** if at least one mix is (1) a failure, (2) **reproducible**: the identical result on 3 reruns,
  and (3) **novel**: it fails a line (mid-play entry or high-leverage leave) that all 12 grid mixes left at 0
  on that same night.
- Otherwise **null**, stated with the budget spent.
- Each reproducible find is written up as a `tests/golden/` candidate (grading rule 7): night, mix, pinned
  counts, target 0. Adding to `tests/golden/` is the integrator's call, not this PR's.

**Reported with CIs** (percentile bootstrap over nights, 2000 draws; PENDING under 10 nights): share of
nights where the grid breaks, where the search breaks, and where a novel reproducible find exists. These
rates are descriptive; the bar is (b)'s existence claim.

## Amendment 1 (before any (b) result was seen)

The first (b) run was stopped after 20 minutes with **no night finished and no output printed**: an 8-game
NBA slate costs about 30-50 s per evaluation on the Mac (background priority, live app running), so the plan
above would have taken 7+ hours. Bar (b) is unchanged; only the night set and budget shrink, chosen blind to
outcomes, by slate size:

- **Nights:** every dev night (train + valid, fit split) with **2-5 games**, NBA and NHL
  (`--split fit --max-games 5`): NBA 20261020, 20261022, 20261027; NHL 20261019, 20261021, 20261023,
  20261025, 20261026, 20261028 = 9 nights. Big slates (overlapping crunches) are NOT covered: a stated gap.
- **Budget:** **32 search evaluations per night** (288 total), the 12 grid mixes, at most **2** distinct finds
  reproduced per night (3 reruns + 3 neighbouring seeds each). Search seed 0.
- 9 nights < 10, so every rate over nights is PENDING; (b) is an existence claim and is judged as written.

## Known limits, stated up front

- The backtest is seeded, so "3 reruns agree" mostly proves determinism. Seed robustness (3 neighbouring
  seeds) is reported beside it to say whether a find is the mix or one lucky draw.
- The stress model is the backtest's, not the TV's: a find is a candidate, not a proven live failure.
- The search is tuned on nothing: budget, plants and seed are fixed here before the run.
