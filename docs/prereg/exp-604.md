# EXP-604: EXP-603 plus the post-scrub safe-moment re-check (Nov 7, 2026)

Owner T9 (Nick's Nov 6 order, item 5). Integrator freezes before any gated run.

## Why
Item 4 (#378) scrubs the hidden stick with the old game on screen, then runs `_wait_safe` (may_cut: the engine's
entry/leave checks on the delayed picture) before the flip; no safe moment in 25 s drops the switch. EXP-603's
wait arm cut the moment the scrub finished, without that re-check, so a cut that waited ~41 s for tune + scrub
landed wherever the wait ended (#378: NFL 115 mid-play/run, all from those cuts).

## Model (on top of EXP-603, flag `hs_recheck`, `eval_sports.py --hs-recheck`, wait arm only; off = EXP-603 byte for byte)
- After a wait, step 1 s at a time from the end of the wait: the cut goes at the first moment the target's delayed
  picture is at a dead ball **as the app sees it**: only plays its feed delivered by then (feed lag = the league's
  `timing.behind_live_s.adaptive.feed_lag_p95_s` with `--real-lag`, else the run's feed_lag) and the app's believed
  lag, not the true one (so S2's ±3.4 s error stresses it). A screen moment past the last delivered play is never
  called safe.
- Live drops a switch with no safe moment in 25 s and the decider picks again; modelled as waiting on (counted
  `late_over_25s`). None within 145 s: cut where the wait ended (counted `none_in_145s`).
- Only the entry is re-checked (Rule #1); the leave is graded as before.
