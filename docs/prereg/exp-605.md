# EXP-605: NBA with YouTube TV buffered and Prime planned at its live edge (Nov 7, 2026)

Owner T9 (Nick's Nov 7 order: EXP-604, then "YouTube TV-only NBA arm", then decide Prime). Integrator freezes first.

## Why
EXP-603/604 NBA failed only through Prime: ~21 of ~22 mid-play entries per run were Prime switches airing at the
live edge while the app planned them B s behind (calibrate_targets gives every app the buffered belief). YouTube
TV-only mid-play was 2.0 -> 1.3 per run (better than control). Prime can't scrub (and pause doesn't hold), so the
honest model of "buffer YouTube TV only" is: the app plans Prime at its live edge.

## Code facts this rests on (integration/next 4dc5d87b)
- The app's planned delay for a target is the channel's fresh-tune (live-edge) delay plus what that tune is held
  or rewound: `decider/screen.py` L252-254 `cal[0] + d.behind` (and `D_edge + label` when labelled, L247-248).
- `held` is the controller's: `engine.py` L149 `self.delays.held = self.control.held`; a fresh tune sets it to 0,
  `control.py` L1475 ("a fresh tune plays from the live edge"); a hold sets it to what the stick measured,
  `control._held_for` L1589 (stick clock vs position; Prime reports a wall-clock position).
- Prime is never scrubbed (#378: "Prime keeps today's path (no scrub on Prime)"); a multiview tile gets 0 s,
  `control._buffer_for` L2912-2922; a full-screen Prime pause hold is measured, not assumed.
So a Prime target nobody has held is planned at its live edge in the live code. EXP-601..604 gave every app the
buffered belief (calibrate_targets), which over-states Prime; this EXP models the code, it does not lift a
registered pessimism about unbuffered YouTube TV switches (those keep the buffered belief, as before).

  picture as if buffered.
