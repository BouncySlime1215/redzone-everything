## Question

Does a pre-game Game Importance model (GI v2) order a night's games by how much people cared about them better
than the closest-game rule?

## Model (frozen at registration)

- Code: `scripts/game_importance.py --target volume` at commit ddafcb8 (branch `claude/wv-aux-source`, #317).
- Model file: `nfl_volume_model.txt`, sha256 prefix `9bea7951b75726b2`, seed 1700, kept privately with the
  crew G artifacts. No refit during this EXP.
- Features: Data B pre-game shards only, as of kickoff or earlier: stakes, schedule and rest, broadcast,
  availability, weather, season (column groups in `GROUPS`).
- Trained on NFL 2024 and 2025 seasons with eval-data locked nights removed. The 77-game 2026 dev set it was
  checked on is spent and is **not** part of this grade.

## Target (label only, never an input)

Within-night rank of log per-game prediction-market volume (crew D, Polymarket public data, derived per-game
totals). Volume measures how much people engaged with a game. It is not a betting signal.

## Arms

| Arm | Score per game |
|---|---|
| **G0 closest game** | −abs(Elo difference) before kickoff (the baseline Nick's question names) |
| **G1 GI v2** | the frozen model above |
| G2 stakes swing (report only) | sum of both teams' playoff-odds swing |

## Metric and bar

- **Metric:** pick agreement = for each night, the share of game pairs that the arm orders the same way as the
  realized volume (ties count half), averaged over nights.
- **Nights:** fresh NFL nights from the integrator's harness freeze onward, excluding every eval-data locked
  night. Each night needs ≥ 2 games with volume.
- **Minimum:** ≥ 8 nights and ≥ 60 game pairs. Below that, the verdict is PENDING.
- **PASS:** G1 − G0 ≥ +3.0 points, and the night-cluster bootstrap 95% CI (2000 resamples, seed 1715) lower bound
  is > 0. **FAIL:** CI upper bound < 0. Anything else: NO DIFFERENCE.
- NBA follows the same plan once crew D's NBA volume history lands and a NBA model is frozen under a new id.
- A PASS only allows a follow-up EXP that puts GI into the decider behind a flag (graded on % of oracle with
  0 mid-play entries). This EXP changes nothing on the TV.

## Leakage checks

- Features are timestamped at or before kickoff (the script drops anything later).
- Locked nights are removed by the list in the crew G share (`locked_nights.json`, from the pin's
  `league_split` test set). The script refuses to train without it.
- The target is never a feature. Market prices and lines are not used here at all.
